# Income Tax Act 1961 — Key Provisions (Sample)

## Section 2 — Definitions

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires:

**"Assessee"** means a person by whom any tax or any other sum of money is payable under this Act, and includes every person in respect of whom any proceeding under this Act has been taken for the assessment of his income.

**"Assessment Year"** means the period of twelve months commencing on the 1st day of April every year.

**"Previous Year"** means the financial year immediately preceding the assessment year.

**"Person"** includes an individual, a Hindu undivided family, a company, a firm, an association of persons or a body of individuals, a local authority, and every artificial juridical person.

## Section 4 — Charge of Income Tax

Where any Central Act enacts that income-tax shall be charged for any assessment year at any rate or rates, income-tax at that rate or those rates shall be charged for that year in accordance with, and subject to the provisions of, this Act in respect of the total income of the previous year of every person.

## Section 10 — Incomes Not Included in Total Income

In computing the total income of a previous year of any person, any income falling within any of the following clauses shall not be included:
- Agricultural income
- Any sum received by a member of a Hindu undivided family out of the income of the family
- Any income of a local authority which does not provide basic minimum services
- Share of profit from a partnership firm (already taxed at firm level)

## Section 44AD — Presumptive Taxation for Small Businesses

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in sections 28 to 43C, in the case of an eligible assessee engaged in an eligible business having total turnover or gross receipts not exceeding two crore rupees in a previous year, a sum equal to eight per cent of the total turnover or gross receipts, or a sum higher than the aforesaid sum claimed to have been earned by the eligible assessee, shall be deemed to be the profits and gains of such business.

For digital receipts (received through banking channels, UPI, or electronic means), the presumptive income rate is six per cent instead of eight per cent.

## Section 80C — Deduction in Respect of Life Insurance Premia, etc.

In computing the total income of an assessee being an individual or a Hindu undivided family, there shall be deducted, the whole of the amount paid or deposited in the previous year, not exceeding one lakh fifty thousand rupees, in:
- Life insurance premium
- Contribution to Provident Fund
- Subscription to National Savings Certificate
- Tuition fees for children (maximum two children)
- Repayment of housing loan principal
- Equity Linked Saving Scheme (ELSS) mutual funds
- Sukanya Samriddhi Account

## Section 139 — Return of Income

Every person being a company or a firm shall furnish a return of his income. Every person other than a company or a firm, if his total income exceeds the maximum amount which is not chargeable to income-tax, shall furnish a return of income on or before the due date.

The due dates for filing income tax returns are:
- 31st July for individuals (non-audit cases)
- 31st October for businesses requiring audit
- 30th November for businesses requiring transfer pricing audit

## Section 194C — TDS on Payment to Contractors

Any person responsible for paying any sum to any resident for carrying out any work (including supply of labour for carrying out any work) in pursuance of a contract shall, at the time of credit of such sum to the account of the contractor or at the time of payment thereof in cash or by issue of a cheque or draft or by any other mode, deduct an amount equal to:
- One per cent where the payment is being made to an individual or HUF
- Two per cent where the payment is being made to any other person

No deduction shall be made where the payment does not exceed thirty thousand rupees in a single payment or one lakh rupees in aggregate during the financial year.

## Section 271 — Penalty for Failure to Furnish Returns, etc.

If the Assessing Officer is satisfied that any person has concealed the particulars of his income or furnished inaccurate particulars, he may direct that such person shall pay by way of penalty a sum which shall not be less than but which shall not exceed three times the amount of tax sought to be evaded.
